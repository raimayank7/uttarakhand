How the code BARB0GOPESH is built

BARB is the bank part: the letters of Bank of Baroda. The fifth character is always the digit 0. GOPESH is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 246012952 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 246 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 246401), 012 is the bank and 952 is the branch.
