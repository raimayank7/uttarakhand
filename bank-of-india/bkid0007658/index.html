How the code BKID0007658 is built

BKID is the bank part: the letters of Bank of India. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 007658 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 249013005 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 249 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 249403), 013 is the bank and 005 is the branch.
